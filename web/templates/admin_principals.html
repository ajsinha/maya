People and roles

A permission system nobody can inspect is one people work around. Roles are sets of permissions and a person may hold more than one, so the column that matters is the effective one — what the union actually permits. Some pairs of roles are refused outright: a validator who may also approve is not performing effective challenge, whatever the org chart says.

Define a role

For the job your organisation actually has — a Model Validation Team Lead, a Regional MRM. Every permission is checked against the closed set: a role granting something nothing checks reads as authority and is not. And a role holding both halves of an incompatible pair is refused on the principal who is given it, however it is named — the check is over permissions for exactly that reason.

Add somebody

A principal is a person or a service, and the difference is not cosmetic: a service signs in to nothing and holds no password. Roles are checked against the incompatible pairs before anything is written — a developer who could also approve versions is a first line approving its own work — and the refusal names the pair.
