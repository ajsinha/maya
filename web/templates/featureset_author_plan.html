The point-in-time rule

{{ plan.pit_rule }}

Published as a string an engine implements, and it is the platform's own — the bound is min(label_ts, as_of) because the two clocks refuse different things. label_ts is what the model could have known when the decision was made; as_of is what the platform could have known when the set was built. Taking the earlier of the two is what makes a read at any as_of at or after the label give the same answer, however many restatements arrived in between. {% if plan.outcome_window_days %} The outcome window is {{ plan.outcome_window_days }} days: that is how long after the decision the label is knowable, and it is why this model cannot be judged the week it ships. {% else %} No outcome window is declared, so nothing here says how long the label takes to mature. {% endif %}

The bytes — as at a stated moment

as_of is required and is not defaulted, because an export is a claim about what was known at a moment and a default would make that moment whatever the clock said when somebody happened to ask. A featureset spans several namespaces, so this is a join, and each part is reduced to what was true and known at that moment before the join — the other order pairs each feature's whole history against every other's. Served from the pins above, never from the head of a namespace. An engine wanting parallelism reads the parts and pulls each itself.
