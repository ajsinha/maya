A replacement must weaken assumptions and strengthen guarantees (L-7), and its schemas must be contravariant in inputs and covariant in outputs (L-12) so a consumer written against the old version still type-checks against the new one. Consumers hold a moving name — champion — never a version, which is what lets a version move without anybody redeploying and is exactly why moving one is not a judgement call.
