WHICH RULEBOOK IS BEING APPLIED

Regulatory regimes

A regime is a supervisor's rulebook written down as obligations over a vocabulary of its own. MAYA holds its state in its words, so applying a regime means translating — and a translation that loses a term cannot be trusted to have preserved the obligation. Each regime therefore reports whether its encoding is consistent: whether everything the obligations mention is something MAYA can actually answer. An inconsistent regime refuses to be activated rather than being activated and quietly abstaining.
