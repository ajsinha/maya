What the estate is relying on being true

An assumption is not a limitation. A limitation is a boundary of competence — what a model is — and it does not stop being true. An assumption is a claim about the world the model reads, and it can stop being true while the model is running. So the question here is not what is enforced but what is watched: an assumption with no monitor is one this platform believes on nobody's authority and would not notice becoming false.
