What the estate is not doing, on purpose

A waiver records a control this model is not meeting, who agreed to that, what is being done instead, and — the part that matters — when it ends. Every estate has these and most keep them in a spreadsheet, which is how a temporary exception reaches its fourth year. There are no indefinite waivers here: a window is mandatory and bounded, and a longer exception is renewed, which is a decision somebody takes again rather than one that lapses into permanence.
