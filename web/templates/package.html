The content digest excludes the manifest
The manifest carries the time the pack was cut, so including it would make every pack differ from every other one and destroy the single comparison a reader actually wants: has anything changed since the last one? Digesting the content alone makes that one number to compare rather than a diff of a hundred files.
A gap is written down
Everything the pack could not gather appears in {{ gaps_name }} with the reason. A pack that silently omitted what it could not reach would read as complete, and the reader would have no way to tell a thin model from a thin export. Preview below to read the gaps before you take a copy.
Documents are rendered, not compiled
Compiling is an act — it authors a document and records that it was authored — so cutting a pack monthly would otherwise author four documents a month and twelve of each a year. A pack whose own production changed the record would also differ from the last one for no reason but that somebody had asked for it.
