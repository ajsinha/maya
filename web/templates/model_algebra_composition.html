An input_to edge is checked with refines(output(source), input(target)) against the latest version at each end, and a drawing that does not type-check is refused — the blast radius would follow it and the composite would have no defined schema. calibrated_by, challenger_of and benchmark_for are deliberately not type-checked: a calibration solves parameters rather than handing an output to an input, and a challenger that counted as a dependency would inflate every blast radius it appeared in.

The composite, computed
A composed network's input schema is derived: the source's inputs, plus everything the target reads that this edge does not carry. A composite whose schema somebody wrote down is a composite that can disagree with its parts.
The contract is computed the same way and answers the harder question: under what conditions does the pair still promise anything? A boundary the target assumes and the source's guarantee implies is settled inside the pair. One the source speaks to and does not imply only looks settled, and is reported separately.
