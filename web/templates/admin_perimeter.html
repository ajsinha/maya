A share is not an examiner portal and establishes no identity. It is a time-boxed link to a content digest — never a path, because a link to a location serves whatever is at that location later. Refused reads are recorded alongside served ones: in a year, the link had expired is a fact somebody will need. {{ share_posture.detail }}
