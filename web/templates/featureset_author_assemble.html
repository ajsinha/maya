The point-in-time rule this assembly obeys

{{ pit_rule }}

Two clocks, and they refuse different things. event_ts is when a fact was true; ingest_ts is when the platform learned it. A row is admitted only if it was true by the label's timestamp and knowable by min(label_ts, as_of) — so a figure that was true in March and restated in August is read at its March value in a March training row, whatever month the set is built in. That min is the reproducibility guarantee: every as_of at or after the label gives the same answer.

The spine
One row per decision: the entity, and the moment its outcome became known. The label column itself comes from the pinned label view, not from here — what you supply is when, because that is the bound every feature read is made under.
Assemble

Assembly is not best effort. Layer 1 refuses outright if either temporal bound is missing — that is a proof, not a warning — and layer 2 independently recomputes a stratified sample by a different route and compares, because an independent recomputation is what stops a bug hiding behind itself. Both answers come back below, and the snapshot records them whichever way they go.
