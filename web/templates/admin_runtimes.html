Runtimes and fibres

One question asked twice. The grammar is what a warrant may say: the verbs, the runtimes that may host a kernel, where inputs may be bound from and where outputs may be written. The fibration is what each trainability class must carry once it is admitted — its evidence, its lifecycle, its metrics. A class with a missing facet is one the platform would answer questions about by omitting them, so the gaps are listed first and start-up refuses on them.

What this deployment may extend
The dividing line is whether the extension changes a governance answer. A notification channel changes how somebody is told; a runtime adapter changes what may execute. Four axes are open and four are closed, and each closed one names what the closure protects — because an axis closed with no reason recorded is one somebody will open.

A closed axis is not a missing feature; it is the feature. A governance platform that can be extended into permissiveness is one whose assurances mean whatever the last plugin author thought.
