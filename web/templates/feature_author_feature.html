Acts on this feature

Seal it

Declares it final. It can still be composed from — that is what sealing is for. An amendment afterwards is refused, and only an administrator can break a seal.

Hand it on

The owner changes; the creator does not. Who made it is history and history does not move.

Withdraw it

Only an ephemeral feature is destroyed. A durable one that something composed from cannot simply stop existing, and the refusal says so. The rows go; the record of the destruction does not.
