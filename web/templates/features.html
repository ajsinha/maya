Load values into a view

The whole upload becomes one feature view version, because a version is what a featureset pins and half a version is not something anybody can pin. Every row must carry entity_id and both clocks — event_ts when the fact was true and ingest_ts when it became known — or the upload is refused. A file missing the second clock cannot be read point-in-time, and guessing it is how the future gets into a training set.

CSV, JSONL, Parquet or Arrow. The format is taken from the extension.
