Model packages

A pack is everything about one model, digested and zipped, for somebody who will not be given a login: a supervisor, an internal auditor, an acquirer's diligence team. Everything about its shape follows from that audience. They cannot query the platform, so a pack is self-contained. They cannot take the platform's word for it, so every file is digested and the manifest is digested over the files. They will read it months later, so it records the evidence chain head it was cut against — which is what makes "has anything changed since?" a question with an answer rather than an assurance.

And they must be able to tell a gap from a silence, so an absence is written down rather than omitted. Pack layout version {{ pack_version }}.
