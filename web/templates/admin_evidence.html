Evidence integrity

Two questions, and they are not the same question. The chain can be checked against itself, which tells you nothing has been edited carelessly — but a chain rewritten from the first node onwards passes that check perfectly. The anchors are heads copied to write-once storage outside the database; comparing against them is the only check here that somebody holding the database cannot simply satisfy.
