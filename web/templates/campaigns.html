Rounds of asking, and what arrives before a model

The population is frozen, and the derivation is kept

A campaign whose population is a live query silently changes size. A model retired in week three turns 47 of 50 into 47 of 49, and the completion figure goes up without anybody having done anything — and completion is the one number a campaign exists to produce. So completion is measured against the population as it stood at launch, coverage against re-running the derivation, and the drift between them is reported rather than folded in.

A proposal is not a model

It has no version, no artifact and nothing that could resolve. Keeping it in the register would be the fastest way to turn one into an inventory of ideas. And the most valuable answer triage gives is this is not a model — the pressure runs entirely the other way, because nobody is ever criticised for registering something.
