Seven states. The whole design turns on one distinction: an attested record is immutable — no field changes, and no new versions — and a mutable one is a record somebody has explicitly opened for change and will have to attest again. Immutability is a state rather than a flag because the question is not “is this locked” but “what is in force, who said so, and when did they say it”.
