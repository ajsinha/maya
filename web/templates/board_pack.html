A limit carries a rationale because a number nobody can explain is a number nobody will change — so it is either ignored or obeyed without thought, and both are worse than not having it. Versions accumulate and nothing is edited: a limit that can be changed without a record is one that can be relaxed without one, and the relaxation is the event a reader six months later comes looking for.
