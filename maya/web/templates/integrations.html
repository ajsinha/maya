Connectors

Models trained elsewhere come in as black-box drafts, with where they came from sealed into the version; they then go through review, warrants and blind scoring like any other model. MAYA's lineage goes out as OpenLineage events. SQL sources, including Snowflake and Databricks, are under Admin → Sources. These connectors are tested against the documents each system publishes, not yet against a live account.

MLflow registry sync

MAYA does not serve models; the platform that does reads MAYA's decisions from the registry. Every MLflow version imported from a registered model carries the alias maya-live while one of its execution warrants is live, and loses it when the warrant is suspended, revoked or expires, or its periodic review falls overdue. This runs every five minutes on its own.

OpenLineage

Every lineage edge, grouped by what it produces, as an OpenLineage RunEvent: one job per produced object, its sources as inputs. Download them, or send them to the endpoint in integrations.openlineage.url.
