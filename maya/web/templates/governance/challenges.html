Champion & challenger

Two training warrants drawn on the same escrowed holdout are scored on exactly the same rows. MAYA compares their errors row by row: the difference in the metric, a paired bootstrap 95% interval for it, and how often the challenger's error is the smaller. Each scoring counts as a holdout attempt on its warrant. The decision is recorded by somebody who does not own the challenger.
