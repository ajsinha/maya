{% from "help/_macros.html" import step, concept, tip, warn, example, fields, screen, cards %} {% call concept('Three words carry most of MAYA') %}

A definition says how a number is produced. A version freezes the definition — how. A pin freezes the resolved data — what. Almost every other term below is a way of governing, deriving from, or proving one of those three.

{% endcall %} {{ cards([ ('pencil-square', 'Definition', 'Editable while a draft; describes index, schema, source, rules, transforms and quality.'), ('check2-circle', 'Version', 'Minted on submit, identified by a definition hash, never edited once approved.'), ('pin-angle', 'Pin', 'Resolved, quality-checked, content-addressed data, sealed and citable forever.'), ]) }}
